# Academic and research-use notice

This project is a research and educational artifact for studying game security,
computer vision, and human/agent behavior in controlled offline environments.

## Strictly prohibited

Do not use this project, its code, models, data, or derivatives to:

- cheat, automate aim, or gain an unfair advantage in online multiplayer games;
- bypass, evade, or interfere with anti-cheat systems;
- send unauthorized input to another person's machine or account;
- violate game terms of service, platform rules, or applicable law;
- conduct surveillance, harassment, or any illegal activity;
- redistribute captured game footage, account data, or third-party assets
  without authorization.

## Permitted scope

Use only in controlled, local, offline environments where you have the right to
run the game and capture the screen, for example:

- offline CS2/Workshop sessions;
- local replay and perception evaluation;
- academic coursework, research, or security education;
- controlled lab experiments with informed participants.

You are responsible for complying with the game's terms, local law, privacy
rules, and any institutional review requirements.

## Data and privacy

Captured frames may contain account names, player names, chat, or other
personal information. The project owner reviewed the existing contact sheets
and reports that they contain no account UI, player names, or chat. That is a
privacy finding, not a copyright or distribution permission.

Raw captures, sample frames, contact sheets, labels, and local paths remain
excluded from the public whitelist by default. They may contain protected game
visuals and are not needed for a defense/research disclosure.
